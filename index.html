The Briefing

Health & safety

☔

August is monsoon

Expect 8–15 rainy days. Afternoon thunderstorms 2–5 PM are normal. The itinerary is rain-proof — most of it is indoors or covered.

🦟

Dengue is real

Long-sleeve at dusk. DEET 20%+ on her skin. The hotel rooms have screens, but the deck is open.

🌊

Beach safety

Pattaya beach has red-flag days during monsoon. If you see red flags, don't go in — the hotel pool is better anyway. Beach shoes essential.

🍜

Eat safely

Eat where locals eat but where the food is hot. Skip raw shellfish. Bottled water only for her. Pattaya tap water is not drinkable.
