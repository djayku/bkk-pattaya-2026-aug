Field Notes · The Important Day

Tomorrow's the day she'll remember when she's 30. The dolphin isn't the destination — the look on her face is.

— From the trip brief
07:00

Slow breakfast — beachfront

Let her watch the boats. The Infinity pool is open from 6am if she wants a pre-show swim.

10:30

🐬 Arrival at Pattaya Dolphinarium

15-min taxi from the hotel. Arrive 30 min early to check in, meet the trainers, and let her acclimatize. Photo-friendly, no flash allowed during the show.

⭐ The moment 12.9563° N, 100.8890° E
11:00

Dolphin & sea lion show

Around 45 min. Music, jumps, ball tricks, splash zone. She'll be in the front row if you ask. Loud volume — bring her mini-earphones or sit at the back if she's sound-sensitive.

12:00

🐬 The swim — bottlenose encounter

This is the trip. You'll be in chest-deep water with her. The dolphin will come up to you, kiss her cheek, let her touch its dorsal fin. Two trainers in the water with you. Photos are taken by the venue and sold after — usually ฿500–800 for the digital package.

⚠️ Confirm 5-yo eligibility with venue first

Tomorrow she'll know: dolphins have names for each other (signature whistles), recognize themselves in mirrors, and surface to breathe every 2 minutes.

13:30

Lunch — back to hotel, check out

Light lunch (she'll be tired). Pack the car and head to Bangkok by 14:30.

14:30 – 16:30

Pattaya → Bangkok

Same motorway, reverse direction. Check in at Siam Kempinski late afternoon.

17:00

Siam Kempinski — pool time

Family Suite has two bathrooms and a balcony. Let her swim in the garden pool while you unpack.

19:00

The Briefing

Health & safety

☔ August is monsoon — plan for it

Expect 8-15 rainy days. Afternoon thunderstorms 2-5pm are normal. The itinerary is rain-proof — most of it is indoors or covered.

🦟 Dengue is real

Long-sleeve at dusk. DEET 20%+ on her skin. The hotel rooms have screens, but the deck is open.

🌊 Beach safety

Pattaya beach has red-flag days during monsoon. If you see red flags, don't go in — the hotel pool is better anyway. Beach shoes are essential.

🍜 Eat safely

Eat where locals eat but where the food is hot. Skip raw shellfish. Bottled water only for her. Pattaya tap water is not drinkable.

☀️ Sun

SPF 50+ every 2 hours. Hat. Sunglasses. Reapply after the swim.
