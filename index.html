🇹🇭 Kid-friendly Thai ordering cheat sheet
• Khao man gai — Hainanese chicken rice (mild, clean)
• Khao pad — fried rice (universal kid win)
• Pad thai — ask for less chili
• Fresh fruit — buy whole, peel yourself
• Coconut water — straight from the nut

Health & Safety

🦟 Dengue — August is peak season
• Apply DEET ≥20% before evening outings (Jodd Fairs, ICONSIAM, beach time)
• Long sleeves at dawn/dusk
• Reapply repellent every 4-6 hours
☀️ August sun + reflection
• SPF 50+ reapply every 2 hours
• Hat + sunglasses for her
• Hydrate constantly — water bottle always in car
🌧️ Monsoon — Aug = 8-15 rainy days
• Showers typically 2-5pm, clear within an hour
• Pack a light poncho for each of you
• Hotel pool + Siam Paragon are built-in rain plans
🏖️ Beach safety
• Stick to InterContinental's private beach · no jet-skis · lifeguards
• Avoid public Pattaya/Jomtien beaches with her
• Stay within arm's reach in the ocean
