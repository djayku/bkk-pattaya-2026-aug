💸 Tipping customs
  • Housekeeping: ฿20–40/night on the pillow
  • Drivers (U-Young × 3 legs): ฿100–200/leg
  • Restaurants: usually no tip (10% service charge auto-added); round up if you want
  • Spa: ฿100–200 on top of bill (not auto-included)
